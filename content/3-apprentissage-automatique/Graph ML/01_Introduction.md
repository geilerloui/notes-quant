
\subsection{Four ways to analyze your Network}
\underline{\textbf{What are the ways to Analyze Networks ?}} \\

\begin{itemize}
    \item Node Classification: Predict the type/color of a given node
    \item Link Prediction: Predict whether two nodes are linked
    \item Community Detection Identify densely linked clusters of nodes
    \item Network similarity: Measure similarity of two nodes/networks
\end{itemize}
